# 题壁画马歌<small class="du-fu-variant">韦偃画，陈浩然、《草堂》本作题壁上韦偃画马歌</small><sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0142-1"><a href="#note-du-fu-0142-1">〔一〕</a></sup>。

<p class="du-fu-verse">韦侯别我有所适，知我怜君<small class="du-fu-variant">一作渠</small>画无敌。<br />戏<small class="du-fu-variant">陈浩然本作试</small>拈秃笔扫骅骝，欻见骐<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-5eaf89ce9114cfbb137b.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.050em" />出东壁<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0142-2"><a href="#note-du-fu-0142-2">〔二〕</a></sup>。<br />一匹龁草一匹嘶，坐看千里当霜蹄。<br />时危安得真致此，与人同生亦同死。</p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0142-1"><span class="footnote-number">〔一〕</span>韦偃：吴若本注：按：张彦远《名画记》：鶠，韦鉴子，善小马、牛、羊、山原，甫他诗皆云偃，未知孰是。甫与偃同时，不应有误，疑《画记》失传。《东观余论》：韦鶠十马后，有李丞相吉甫题字，真佳迹也。少陵有《韦偃画马》诗，偃当作“鶠”，盖传写之误。《阁中集》《名画记》皆作“鶠”。又云：张彦远谓鶠，善画山原、小马、牛、羊，今晋王所藏本皆沛艾，不特善小驷而已。李将军画马神胜形，韩丞画马形胜神，鶠从容二人间，第笔格差不及耳。朱景玄《名画评》：韦偃，京兆人，寓居于蜀，善画山水、竹树、人物等。思高格逸，居闲常以越笔点簇鞍马，千变万态。或腾或倚，或龁或饮，或惊或止，或走或起，或翘或跂。其小者，或头一点，或尾一抹，山以墨斡，水以手擦，曲尽其妙，宛然如真。亦有图骐<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-fafe68958058754a5fff.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.000em" />之良，尽衔勒之饰，巧妙精奇，韩干之匹也。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0142-1" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0142-2"><span class="footnote-number">〔二〕</span>骐<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-1d3adbf45a9638a3bb3d.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.000em" />：吴若本注：“骐<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-c5277a373bd94b9842e2.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.000em" />”字，《说文》不载，惟《玉篇》云：马黑脊。不言良马。往见晁无咎云：既扫骅骝，乃见骐<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-c5277a373bd94b9842e2.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.000em" />。乃是语病，不然必有误。或作“<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-9af450ffea10d503ea25.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 2.050em" />”二字，尤非。甫好用“骐<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-1236dfce5a240e29ccf8.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.200em" />”字，疑别有出云。《骢马行》：“肯使骐<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-9c7cecba99782f9dc4cb.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.000em" />地上行。”《赠李二丈》云：“蹭蹬骐<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-e515df701b23922f5878.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.000em" />老。”<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0142-2" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

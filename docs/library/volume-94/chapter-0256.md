# 槐叶冷淘

<p class="du-fu-verse">青青高槐叶，采掇付中厨。<br />新面来近市，汁滓宛相俱。<br />入鼎资过熟，加餐愁欲无。<br />碧鲜俱照箸，香饭兼苞芦。<br />经齿冷于雪，劝人投此<small class="du-fu-variant">一作比</small>珠。<br />愿随金<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-86bc0b117edc3d7d8eb4.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.050em" />袅，走置锦屠苏<small class="du-fu-variant">又作廜</small><img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-c6bf06f9b5f6907eca00.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.000em" /><sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0256-1"><a href="#note-du-fu-0256-1">〔一〕</a></sup>。<br />路远思恐泥，兴深终不渝。<br />献芹则小小，荐藻明区区。<br />万里露寒殿<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0256-2"><a href="#note-du-fu-0256-2">〔二〕</a></sup>，开冰清玉壶。<br />君王纳凉晚，此味亦时须。</p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0256-1"><span class="footnote-number">〔一〕</span>屠苏：吴若本注云：庵也。《博雅》：廜<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-8569c41346047f4c3b3e.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.000em" />，庵也。服虔《通俗文》：屋平曰屠<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-5b8d3633305a857ed0d2.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.000em" />。刘孝威《结客少年场行》：“插腰铜匕首，障日锦屠苏。”或以为屠苏酒。非是。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0256-1" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

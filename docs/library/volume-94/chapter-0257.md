# 行官张望补稻畦水归

<p class="du-fu-verse">东屯大江北<small class="du-fu-variant">一云枕大江，</small>百顷平若案。<br />六月青稻多，千畦碧泉乱。<br />插秧适云已，引溜加溉灌。<br />更仆往方塘，决渠当断岸。<br />公私各地著，浸润无天旱。<br />主守问家臣，分明<small class="du-fu-variant">一作朋</small>见溪伴<small class="du-fu-variant">一作畔。</small>芊芊<small class="du-fu-variant">一作芋芋，一作竿竿</small>炯翠羽，剡剡生<small class="du-fu-variant">一作向</small>银汉。<br />鸥鸟镜里来，关山雪边看。<br />秋菰成黑米，精凿<small class="du-fu-variant">一作谷</small>传<small class="du-fu-variant">一作傅</small>白粲。<br />玉粒足晨炊<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0257-1"><a href="#note-du-fu-0257-1">〔一〕</a></sup>，红鲜任霞散<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0257-2"><a href="#note-du-fu-0257-2">〔二〕</a></sup>。<br />终然添旅食，作苦期壮观。<br />遗穗及众多，我仓戒滋蔓。</p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0257-1"><span class="footnote-number">〔一〕</span>玉粒：《拾遗记》：山名环丘，上有方湖千里，多大鹄，高一丈。群飞于湖际，采不周之粟于环丘之上。生穟高五丈，其粒皎然如玉。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0257-1" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0257-2"><span class="footnote-number">〔二〕</span>红鲜：鲜于注：江浙人谓江米曰红鲜。又曰：红鲜谓鱼色之鲜如霞也，淮南、吴越人有此。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0257-2" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

# 王兵马使二角鹰<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0288-1"><a href="#note-du-fu-0288-1">〔一〕</a></sup>

<p class="du-fu-verse">悲台萧飒<small class="du-fu-variant">一作瑟</small>石<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-3ad6832b67bcd37fb0f7.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 0.950em" /><img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-a8904165fac3cfeb0c34.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 1.050em" />，哀壑杈枒浩呼<small class="du-fu-variant">刊作污</small>汹。<br />中有万里之长江，回风滔<small class="du-fu-variant">陈作陷</small>日孤光动。<br />角鹰翻倒壮士臂，将军玉帐轩翠<small class="du-fu-variant">一云昂，一云勇</small>气<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0288-2"><a href="#note-du-fu-0288-2">〔二〕</a></sup>。<br />二鹰猛脑徐侯穟，<small class="du-fu-variant">荆作绦徐坠。赵云：徐侯穟，殊无理义，介甫善本作绦徐坠，于理或然。</small>目如愁胡视天地<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0288-3"><a href="#note-du-fu-0288-3">〔三〕</a></sup>。<br />杉鸡竹兔不自惜，溪<small class="du-fu-variant">一作孩</small>虎野羊俱辟易。<br />鞲上锋棱十二翮<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0288-4"><a href="#note-du-fu-0288-4">〔四〕</a></sup>，将军勇锐与之敌。<br />将军树勋起安西，昆仑虞泉入马蹄。<br />白羽曾肉三狻猊<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0288-5"><a href="#note-du-fu-0288-5">〔五〕</a></sup>，敢决岂不与之齐。<br />荆南芮公得将军，亦如角鹰下翔<small class="du-fu-variant">一作入朔</small>云。<br />恶鸟飞飞啄金屋，安得尔辈开其群，驱出六合枭鸾分。</p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0288-1"><span class="footnote-number">〔一〕</span>角鹰：《酉阳杂俎》：雕角鹰等，四月一日停放，五月上旬置笼。《埤雅》：鹰鹞，二年之色也，顶有角，毛微起，今通谓之角鹰。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0288-1" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0288-2"><span class="footnote-number">〔二〕</span>翠气：《甘泉赋》：飏翠气之宛延。善曰：言宫观之高，故翠气宛延，在其侧而扬之。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0288-2" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0288-3"><span class="footnote-number">〔三〕</span>愁胡：孙楚《鹰赋》：深目蛾眉，状似愁胡。魏彦《深鹰赋》：立如植木，望似愁胡。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0288-3" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0288-4"><span class="footnote-number">〔四〕</span>十二翮：傅玄《鹰赋》：左看若侧，右视如倾，劲翮二六，机连体轻。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0288-4" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0288-5"><span class="footnote-number">〔五〕</span>狻猊：《尔雅》：狻麑如虦猫，食虎豹。注：狮子也。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0288-5" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

# 曲江二首

## 其一

<p class="du-fu-verse">一片花飞减却春，风飘万点正愁人。<br />且看欲尽花经眼，莫厌伤多酒入唇。<br />江上小堂<small class="du-fu-variant">川作棠</small>巢翡翠，花<small class="du-fu-variant">一作苑</small>边高冢卧麒麟<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0435-1"><a href="#note-du-fu-0435-1">〔一〕</a></sup>。<br />细推物理须行乐，何用<small class="du-fu-variant">晋作事</small>浮名<small class="du-fu-variant">一作荣</small>绊此身。</p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0435-1"><span class="footnote-number">〔一〕</span>麒麟：《西京杂记》：五柞宫前有青梧观，观前有三梧桐树，足下有石麒麟二枚，刊其胁为文字，是始皇骊山墓上物也。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0435-1" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

## 其二

<p class="du-fu-verse">朝回日日典春衣，每日江头尽醉归。<br />酒债寻常行处有<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0435-2"><a href="#note-du-fu-0435-2">〔一〕</a></sup>，人生七十古来稀。<br />穿花蛱蝶深深见<small class="du-fu-variant">一作舞，</small>点水蜻蜓款款<small class="du-fu-variant">一云缓缓</small>飞。<br />传语风光共流转，暂时相赏莫相违。</p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0435-2"><span class="footnote-number">〔一〕</span>酒债：孔融诗：还家酒债多，门客粲成行。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0435-2" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

<h1>促织</h1>

<p class="du-fu-verse">促织甚微细，哀音何动人。<br />草根吟不稳，床下夜相亲。<br />久客得无泪，放妻难及晨。<br />悲丝与急管，感激异天真。</p>

# 促织<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0483-1"><a href="#note-du-fu-0483-1">〔一〕</a></sup>

<p class="du-fu-verse">促织甚微细，哀音<small class="du-fu-variant">一作声</small>何动人。<br />草根吟<small class="du-fu-variant">一作冷</small>不稳，床下夜相亲。<br />久客得无泪，放<small class="du-fu-variant">一作故</small>妻难及晨。<br />悲丝<small class="du-fu-variant">一云弦</small>与急管，感激异天真。</p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0483-1"><span class="footnote-number">〔一〕</span>促织：《释虫》：蟋蟀，蛬。注：今促织也。陆机疏云：蟋蟀一名蛬，一名青<img class="du-fu-glyph" src="/library/volume-94/images/glyph-ac0d152372ffdd8a04f4.jpg" alt="原书用字" style="width: 0.950em" />。楚人谓之王孙，幽州人谓之趋织。里语曰：趋织鸣，懒妇惊。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0483-1" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

<h1>田舍</h1>

# 田舍

<p class="du-fu-verse">田舍清江曲<small class="du-fu-variant">一作上，</small>柴门古道旁。<br />草深迷市井，地僻懒衣裳。<br />榉<small class="du-fu-variant">唐顾陶作杨</small>柳枝枝弱<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0523-1"><a href="#note-du-fu-0523-1">〔一〕</a></sup>，枇杷树树<small class="du-fu-variant">顾陶作对对</small>香。<br />鸬鹚西日照，晒翅满鱼梁。</p>

<p class="footnote-definition footnote-source" id="note-du-fu-0523-1"><span class="footnote-number">〔一〕</span>榉柳：《本草衍义》：榉木皮，今人呼为榉柳，然叶谓柳非柳，谓槐非槐。吴曾《漫录》：今本作榉柳，非也，枇杷一物，榉柳则二物矣，对对亦胜树树也。<span class="footnote-backrefs"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#note-ref-du-fu-0523-1" aria-label="返回正文">↩</a></span></p>

<p class="du-fu-verse">客里有所过<small class="du-fu-variant">一作适，</small>归来知路难。<br />开门野鼠走，散帙壁鱼干<sup class="footnote-ref" id="note-ref-du-fu-0742-1"><a href="#note-du-fu-0742-1">〔一〕</a></sup>。<br />洗杓开新酝，低头拭小盘<small class="du-fu-variant">一云着小冠。</small>凭谁给曲蘖，细酌老江干。</p>

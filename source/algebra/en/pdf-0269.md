**14.** Let $\operatorname{char}K=p$. Let $L$ be a finite extension of $K$, and suppose $[L:K]$ prime to $p$. Show that $L$ is separable over $K$.

**15.** Suppose $\operatorname{char}K=p$. Let $a\in K$. If $a$ has no $p$-th root in $K$, show that $X^{p^n}-a$ is irreducible in $K[X]$ for all positive integers $n$.

**16.** Let $\operatorname{char}K=p$. Let $\alpha$ be algebraic over $K$. Show that $\alpha$ is separable if and only if $K(\alpha)=K(\alpha^{p^n})$ for all positive integers $n$.

**17.** Prove that the following two properties are equivalent:

**14.** Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over an algebraically closed field $k$. Let $A\in\operatorname{End}_k(E)$. Show that $A$ can be written in a unique way as a sum

[*Hint:* Let $P_A(t)=\prod(t-\lambda_i)^{m_i}$ be the factorization of $P_A(t)$ with distinct $\lambda_i$. Let $E_i$ be the kernel of $(A-\lambda_i)^{m_i}$. Then $E$ is the direct sum of the $E_i$.

By hypothesis, $H_{E_i}$ is an isomorphism. It then follows trivially that $H_E$ is an isomorphism. For instance, to prove injectivity, we write an element $v\in L(E)$ in the form

$$
v=\sum L(\varphi_i)v_i,
$$

with $v_i\in L(E_i)$. If $H_Ev=0$, then

$$
0=\sum H_EL(\varphi_i)v_i=\sum M(\varphi_i)H_{E_i}v_i,
$$

and since the maps $M(\varphi_i)$ $(i=1,\ldots,m)$ give a direct sum decomposition of $M(E)$, we conclude that $H_{E_i}v_i=0$ for all $i$, whence $v_i=0$, and $v=0$. The surjectivity is equally trivial.

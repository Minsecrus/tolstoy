We now prove GAFT. By Corollary 2.3.7, it is enough to show that $(A\Rightarrow G)$ has an initial object for each $A\in\mathscr{A}$. Let $A\in\mathscr{A}$. By Lemma A.2, $(A\Rightarrow G)$ is complete, and by hypothesis, it has a weakly initial set. It is also locally small, since $\mathscr{B}$ is. Hence by Lemma A.1, it has an initial object, as required.

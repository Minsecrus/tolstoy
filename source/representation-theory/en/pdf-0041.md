**Remark 2.11.4.** Note that a similar definition to the above can be used to define the tensor product $V\otimes_A W$, where $A$ is any ring, $V$ is a right $A$-module, and $W$ is a left $A$-module. Namely, $V\otimes_A W$ is the abelian group which is the quotient of the group $V\bullet W$ freely generated by formal symbols $v\otimes w$, $v\in V$, $w\in W$, modulo the relations

$$
\begin{aligned}
&(v_1+v_2)\otimes w-v_1\otimes w-v_2\otimes w,\\
&v\otimes(w_1+w_2)-v\otimes w_1-v\otimes w_2,\\
&va\otimes w-v\otimes aw,\qquad a\in A.
\end{aligned}
$$
